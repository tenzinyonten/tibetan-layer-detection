You are an expert cataloguer of Tibetan Buddhist literature. Classify the book below into ONE primary category: liturgy, prayer, sadhana, songs, or other. If you choose "other", you must specify its exact sub-genre. Base your decision on the title, opening excerpt, middle excerpt, and colophon.

PRIMARY CATEGORIES
1. liturgy: Ritual and ceremonial manuals, offering liturgies, empowerment rites, fire pujas, purification ceremonies.
   Key markers: ཆོ་ག (rite), དབང་བསྐུར (empowerment), སྦྱིན་སྲེག (fire offering), མཆོད་པ (offering), ལས་རིམ (ritual sequence), ཆོས་སྤྱོད (liturgical recitation).
2. prayer: Devotional supplications, aspirations, praises, and dedications in verse.
   Key markers: གསོལ་འདེབས (supplication), སྨོན་ལམ (aspiration), བསྟོད་པ (praise), བཀྲ་ཤིས (auspicious verses), བརྒྱུད་འདེབས (lineage prayer). High verse-like percentage (>55%).
3. sadhana: Deity-yoga practice scripts meant for active meditation/recitation with visualization and mantra sequences.
   Key markers: སྒྲུབ་ཐབས (means of accomplishment), བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ, བསྐྱེད་རིམ, རྫོགས་རིམ, བླ་མའི་རྣལ་འབྱོར.
4. songs: Spiritual poetry of realization, dohas, and experiential songs.
   Key markers: མགུར (spiritual song), གསུང་མགུར, ཉམས་མགུར, རྡོ་རྗེའི་གླུ (vajra song), དོ་ཧ (doha). Typically 1st-person reflection.
5. other: Scholastic texts, instructional guides, philosophical treatises, histories, biographies, or compendiums that do not fit the 4 performative genres above.

SUB-GENRES FOR "OTHER" (Choose exactly ONE if category is "other", else "none"):
- commentary: Word-by-word or section commentaries (འགྲེལ་པ, རྣམ་བཤད, མཆན་འགྲེལ, ཊཱིཀྐ).
- instruction_manual: Discursive guidance on how to meditate or practice (ཁྲིད་ཡིག, ཞལ་ཁྲིད, ཟིན་བྲིས, དམིགས་ཁྲིད) without being a scripted recitation text.
- treatise_philosophy: Root philosophical texts, logic, Abhidharma, Madhyamaka, Vinaya treatises (གཞུང་, བསྟན་བཅོས, རྣམ་གཞག).
- biography_history: Hagiographies, life stories, or chronicles (རྣམ་ཐར, ལོ་རྒྱུས, ཆོས་འབྱུང).
- collected_works: Multi-text volumes, general compendiums, or collected works lacking one dominant genre (གསུང་འབུམ, གླེགས་བམ).
- poetics_grammar_astrology: Texts on grammar, poetics, astrology, medicine, or secular arts (སྙན་ངག, བརྡ་དག, རྩིས).
